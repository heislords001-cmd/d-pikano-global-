Core service disciplines

What we build and maintain.

Every discipline under one contractor, from first fabrication drawing to commissioning and staff training on site.

01

Fire Fighting Systems

Sprinkler systems, fire hydrant networks, fire hose reel systems, and packaged fire fighting pump installation, including electric, diesel, and jockey pump sets.

02

HVAC & Refrigeration

Chilled water pipe fabrication, duct work, air handling units, chiller installation, and industrial thermal insulation and cladding of chiller pipes for retail and industrial spaces.

03

Industrial Plumbing

Water supply and drainage systems, sanitary appliance installation, water reticulation, bulk storage tank fabrication, overhead tank installation, swimming pool construction, and water treatment plant (WTP), sewage treatment plant (STP), and effluent treatment plant (ETP) installation.

04

Lift Installation

Supply and installation of passenger lift equipment, from 2 to 12 passenger units, commissioned and handed over with full operational training.

05

Pipe Fabrication & Welding

Mild steel, stainless steel, and CPVC pipe fabrication for diesel, LPG, nitrogen, air, and process lines, welded and pressure tested on site.
