Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026. A short, honest explanation of what this website does and doesn't do with cookies.

This website does not use cookies

d-pikano.com does not set its own cookies, run analytics scripts, or use any advertising or tracking technology. There is no visitor tracking on this site.

The one exception: the quote request form

When you submit the "Request A Quote" form, the information you enter (name, contact details, project details) is sent directly to Formspree, a third-party form-processing service, which delivers it to D-Pikano Global Resources Ltd by email. Formspree may use its own cookies or store submission data as part of providing that service, under its own privacy and cookie practices — not ours. You can review those directly at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Browser and hosting logs

Like virtually any website, our hosting provider may keep basic server-level access logs (such as IP address and request timestamps) for security and performance purposes. These aren't cookies and aren't used to track individuals across visits or other websites.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics, advertising, or other tracking tools to this site in the future, this page will be updated to reflect that honestly before those tools go live.
