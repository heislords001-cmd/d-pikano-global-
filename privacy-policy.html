Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information D-Pikano Global Resources Ltd ("we," "us," "our") collects through this website (the "Site") and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you choose to give us, mainly through the quote request form. This may include your name, email address, phone number, company name, project location, and details about the work you need done.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information such as national ID numbers, financial account details, or health information through the Site, and we ask that you avoid including this kind of information in your quote request.

2. How we use your information

We use the information you submit to:

  • Respond to your quote request and discuss your project
  • Prepare estimates and proposals
  • Keep records of enquiries for our own business purposes

We do not sell your information to third parties.

3. How quote requests are handled

Quote requests submitted through the Site are delivered to our team through a third party form service. That service processes the submission only to deliver it to us and is not used to display your information publicly.

4. Cookies and analytics

The Site may use basic, standard web technologies to load fonts and styling. We do not currently use advertising cookies or third party tracking for marketing purposes.

5. How long we keep information

We keep quote request information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to your enquiry and maintain business records, after which it may be deleted.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, by contacting us using the details on our contact section. We will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you give us, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

The Site is intended for businesses and individuals seeking mechanical engineering services and is not directed at children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, please reach out through the details on our contact section.
